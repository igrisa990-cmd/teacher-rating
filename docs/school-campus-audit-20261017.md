# Organizations, school centers and campuses

Database status: applied on 18 October 2026. The schema, 1,742 educational addresses, 34 duplicate redirects, one unrelated map-profile correction and 331 newly verified schools are live. The resulting active catalog contains 41,958 schools and 78,871 teachers. The 79,085 total teacher records and their organization/source mapping digest are unchanged.

The former model had one address per organization. That cannot represent Moscow school 548 or Yaroslavl's reorganized educational complexes. The new child records distinguish school/branch/preschool locations and unclassified educational places, retain source and identity hashes, and keep every teacher/review on its existing organization. An organization's legal address is not automatically a campus.

Verified examples:

- School 548, OGRN 1037739570384: four school campuses in Moscow, two school/branch locations in Moscow Oblast, and four preschool locations. The school's published address list was joined by `unique_address_id` to the platform's organization API with the exact OGRN. The broader 15-address organization inventory was not treated as 15 current school campuses.
- School 99, Yaroslavl: center of complex 6 “Квант”, OGRN 1027600512532, Труфанова, 25а. School 58 is another center at Труфанова, 21а. The current official page links the exact active license record.

Sources: [548 published locations](https://cou548.mskobr.ru/api/get/addresses.json), [548 organization identity](https://api-app.mskobr.ru/api/v3/organization/13866), [complex 6 official information](https://ok6-yar.edu.yar.ru/svedeniya_ob_obrazovatelnoy_organizatsii/osnovnie_svedeniya/index.html), [Yaroslavl department overview](https://yar-edudep.ru/obwee_obrazovanie/).

The local audit traversed all 23,603 collection checkpoints and 8,446 available pages. The reviewed payload contains 1,742 distinct addresses for 321 active, uniquely matched organizations. Moscow's published lists contributed 1,383 locations from 155 organizations; the broader 162-organization inventory is not a publication input. Every source page must independently prove its organization. Network partners, practice sites, ambiguous multi-address strings and shared cross-organization claims are held. Unknown building types remain unknown.

All 3,930 active profiles without organization OGRNs were compared with verified campus evidence. 34 duplicates were soft archived and redirected, with the reported school 548 case individually reviewed because its OSM address omitted corpus 2. One clearly unrelated barber-training profile was also soft archived. All 35 profiles had zero dependent records. The correction function rechecks **all** current foreign keys under row locks, stores a private before-image, archives without deletion, and creates a canonical-link alias for duplicates. A referenced profile aborts the transaction.

Coverage is incomplete. The earlier 15 October source snapshot contained 39,661 active main-organization general-program license records. The new complete 17 October archive contains 307,607 licenses, including 39,655 eligible active current non-branch general-program records. A conservative locality parser recognizes explicit prefix/suffix settlement names and reversed republic names. This produced 331 entirely missing organizations in 28 regions; all were inserted after database preflight found no existing organization or automatic map matches. Their names, addresses, regions, license status and identifiers match the staged official records. No existing organization was updated or attached automatically.

The remaining review queue contains 732 records with unresolved explicit school addresses, 844 outside the school organization classifier and 83 without valid organization OGRNs. These are license records, not a count of missing schools. No national campus census or complete teacher coverage is claimed.

## Deployment and recovery

The saved schema migration was applied atomically through the connected SQL tool after explicit production approval. It was not registered through the CLI migration-history endpoint; do not replay it. New public tables are read-only for anonymous/authenticated users, follow parent-school RLS, and expose no personnel/contact fields. Staging, before-images and privileged import/correction functions remain private. Search retains the existing caller RLS and request bounds, adds campus names/addresses to the spelling cache and respects explicitly proven campus regions. No Auth, SMTP, SMS or moderator changes are included.
